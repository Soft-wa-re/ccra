# Online Communication Guidelines

*(Addendum to the Code of Conduct)*

**Last Updated:** {{ site.last_updated }}

These Online Communication Guidelines apply to digital communication spaces administered by **{{ page.organizer_legal }}**, doing business as **{{ page.group_name }}**, including forums, listservs, email groups, group chats, social media groups, virtual meeting chats, and other online channels (collectively, the “Online Spaces”).

These guidelines operate in conjunction with the Code of Conduct and apply only to Online Spaces managed by the Association.

---

## 1. Purpose of Online Spaces

Online Spaces exist primarily for:

* Association announcements and program coordination
* Professional discussion relevant to compensation, total rewards, benefits, human resources, and related fields
* Committee, volunteer, and member communications
* Sharing information relevant to Association Activities

Online Spaces are not intended to function as general debate forums, open-topic discussion boards, or channels for unauthorized solicitation.

---

## 2. Political and Divisive Topics

To maintain a welcoming and low-conflict environment for participants of diverse backgrounds and viewpoints:

* Partisan political campaigning, advocacy, or debate is not permitted within Online Spaces.
* Content likely to generate divisive or hostile discussion may be removed.
* Repeated attempts to initiate polarizing discussions may result in moderation action within Online Spaces.

This guideline applies regardless of political affiliation or viewpoint.

---

## 3. Off-Topic Posting

Occasional light conversation is welcome. However:

* Repeated off-topic posting that disrupts the primary purpose of Online Spaces may be moderated.
* Spam, promotional content, or unrelated advertising is not permitted without prior approval.

---

## 4. Confidential and Professional Information

Members and participants are responsible for protecting confidential, proprietary, personal, privileged, trade secret, competitively sensitive, and restricted information. Do not post company-specific compensation data, employee information, client information, vendor information, or other restricted material unless you have authority to share it.

Information posted in Online Spaces should not be treated as confidential. Electronic messages may be copied, forwarded, retained, deleted, searched, or disclosed by recipients, platform providers, or the Association.

---

## 5. Excessive Posting

To keep Online Spaces usable for everyone:

* Avoid sending many back-to-back messages in a short time, especially if the content could be combined into one message.
* Avoid “flooding” the chat with multiple photos, videos, or long multi-part updates. When possible, consolidate or use a single post/thread.
* If you have a longer discussion, move it to direct messages (DMs) unless it’s clearly relevant to the broader group.
* Repeated excessive posting after a moderator request to slow down may result in moderation action.

---

## 6. Moderation Authority

The Association reserves the right, at its sole discretion, to moderate Online Spaces, including:

* Removing or editing messages
* Temporarily muting participants
* Removing individuals from Online Spaces
* Restricting posting privileges within Online Spaces

Moderation decisions are made to preserve a respectful, professional environment and are final.

---

## 6. Escalation

Participants who repeatedly violate these guidelines may:

* Receive a warning
* Be temporarily suspended from Online Spaces
* Be removed from Online Spaces
* Be referred to the Board of Directors for membership or participation action

In serious cases, behavior may be referred to appropriate authorities if required by law or safety concerns.

---

## 7. Relationship to Code of Conduct

All expectations outlined in the Code of Conduct apply within Online Spaces.

Participation in Online Spaces constitutes agreement to abide by these guidelines.
