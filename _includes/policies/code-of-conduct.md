# Code of Conduct

**Last Updated:** {{ site.last_updated }}

This Code of Conduct applies to membership, programs, meetings, events, professional development offerings, committee activities, websites, online spaces, and related communications (collectively, “Association Activities”) organized, hosted, promoted, or facilitated by **{{ page.organizer_legal }}**, doing business as **{{ page.group_name }}** (the “Association").

All participants are expected to follow this Code of Conduct as a condition of participation.

---

## 1. Purpose

Association Activities are intended to be welcoming, respectful, and professionally valuable spaces for learning, leadership development, discussion, networking, governance, and information sharing.

This Code of Conduct exists to:

- Set clear expectations for behavior
- Promote safety and inclusivity
- Empower organizers to address issues early
- Protect participants, venues, and the broader community

---

## 2. Expected Behavior

Participants are expected to:

- Treat others with respect, courtesy, and consideration
- Engage in conversations, programs, and committee work in good faith
- Respect personal boundaries and consent
- Be mindful of how their words or actions may impact others
- Follow all applicable laws, venue rules, platform policies, bylaws, and Association policies
- Protect confidential, proprietary, personal, and restricted information
- Avoid unauthorized solicitation, sales activity, or use of Association access for improper personal or commercial purposes
- Drink responsibly if alcohol is present
- Comply with reasonable instructions from Association leaders, volunteers, venue staff, or platform moderators

---

## 3. Unacceptable Behavior

The following behaviors are not permitted at Association Activities or in related online spaces:

- Harassment, intimidation, threats, or discrimination of any kind
- Unwanted sexual attention or advances
- Physical violence or threats of violence
- Excessive intoxication or disruptive behavior
- Illegal activity, including drug use or underage drinking
- Damage to venue property or personal property
- Stalking, coercion, or persistent unwanted contact
- Unauthorized disclosure of confidential, proprietary, personal, privileged, trade secret, or restricted information
- Improper sharing of company-specific compensation, benefits, employee, client, or vendor information
- Unauthorized use of membership lists, attendee lists, or contact information
- Misrepresentation of the Association’s position, endorsement, affiliation, or authority
- Repeated or patterned behavior that creates an unsafe or hostile environment

This list is not exhaustive.

---

## 4. Alcohol and Substances

Some Association Activities may take place at venues where alcohol is available.

Participants are responsible for:

- Monitoring their own consumption
- Making safe and legal choices
- Arranging safe transportation to and from Association Activities

The Association does not provide alcohol unless explicitly stated and does not monitor or manage consumption.

---

## 5. Reporting Concerns

If you experience or witness behavior that violates this Code of Conduct, you are encouraged to:

- Contact an Association leader or program organizer during the Association Activity, or
- Report concerns by contacting the Association at **{{ page.contact_email | default: "president@carolinacompandrewards.org" }}**

Reports will be handled with discretion to the extent reasonably possible.

---

## 6. Enforcement

The Association reserves the right, at its sole discretion and consistent with applicable bylaws and law, to take appropriate action in response to violations of this Code of Conduct, including but not limited to:

- Verbal warnings
- Removal from an Association Activity
- Temporary or permanent limits on participation privileges
- Suspension or termination of access to online spaces
- Referral to the Board of Directors for membership action
- Reporting behavior to venue staff or authorities when appropriate

Failure to comply with this Code of Conduct may result in immediate removal.

---

## 7. No Retaliation

Retaliation against individuals who report concerns or participate in an investigation of a Code of Conduct issue is not permitted.

---

## 8. Relationship to Other Policies

This Code of Conduct operates in conjunction with:

- Terms of Participation
- Privacy Statement
- Assumption of Risk and Release of Liability

Participation in Association Activities constitutes acceptance of all applicable policies.

---

## 9. Acknowledgment

By accessing the website, applying for membership, registering for, attending, or participating in an Association Activity, you acknowledge that you have read, understood, and agree to abide by this Code of Conduct.
