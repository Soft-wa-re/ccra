- Contact information, such as name, email address, phone number, mailing address, employer, title, and professional affiliation

We do not request company confidential information, employee-specific information, nonpublic compensation data, client information, or other restricted information as a condition of membership or participation.

- Communicate about Association Activities, bylaws, elections, committee work, and organizational business
- Analyze website, program, and communication usage in aggregate form
