# Refund and Cancellation Policy

**Last Updated:** {{ site.last_updated }}

This Refund and Cancellation Policy applies to membership dues, registrations, programs, meetings, events, courses, workshops, materials, sponsorships, and related offerings (collectively, “Offerings”) provided by **{{ page.organizer_legal }}**, doing business as **{{ page.group_name }}** (the “Association,” “we,” “us,” or “our”).

By purchasing, registering for, transferring, canceling, attending, or participating in any Offering, you (“Participant,” “Member,” “you,” or “your”) acknowledge and agree to the following.

---

## 1. General Policies

All cancellation, transfer, or refund requests must be submitted by email to **{{ page.contact_email | default: "president@carolinacompandrewards.org" }}**.

Prepayment is required for paid Offerings unless the Association states otherwise in writing. Payments must be made in U.S. dollars by a payment method accepted by the Association or its payment processor.

Prices, fees, registration details, availability, and policies are subject to change without notice. Taxes, processing fees, and transaction fees may apply where required or charged by third-party providers.

---

## 2. Account Credits

When the Association issues an account credit instead of a refund, the credit is valid for two years from the date issued unless a separate written agreement states otherwise.

Credits not used within two years are deemed a contribution to the Association’s ongoing programs, professional development, research, operations, and related activities that support the profession.

Credits have no cash value, may not be sold, and may be transferred only with prior written approval from the Association.

---

## 3. Nonrefundable Items

The following items are nonrefundable after purchase unless the Association states otherwise in writing:

- Individual membership dues
- Subscription-based products or services
- Sponsorships, advertising, exhibit space, or promotional placements
- Downloadable, digital, or recorded content
- Hardcopy books, printed course materials, and shipped materials
- Donations or contributions
- Processing fees, transaction fees, wire fees, and other third-party fees

---

## 4. Programs, Courses, and Workshops

For classroom, virtual classroom, workshop, cohort, and similar professional development programs, cancellation eligibility is based on when the Association receives your cancellation or transfer request by email.

Program fees become nonrefundable and nontransferable once you access an online course portal, digital course environment, program materials, or similar restricted content.

| Cancellation Email Receipt | Cancellation Eligibility |
| ----- | ----- |
| More than 30 calendar days before the program start date | Receive a full refund, account credit, or transfer to another eligible program or participant. |
| 15-30 calendar days before the program start date | Receive account credit or transfer to another eligible program or participant. |
| 14 or fewer calendar days before the program start date | Fees are not refundable or transferable. |

---

## 5. E-Learning, Recorded, and On-Demand Offerings

Fees for e-learning, recorded, on-demand, self-paced, or similar digital Offerings become nonrefundable and nontransferable 30 days after purchase or once you access the online portal, recording, digital materials, or restricted content, whichever occurs first.

---

## 6. Exams, Assessments, and Credentials

Exam, assessment, certification, credential, or similar fees become nonrefundable and nontransferable 30 days after purchase unless the applicable Offering states a different policy in writing.

---

## 7. Events, Meetings, and Conferences

Event, meeting, and conference cancellation policies may vary by Offering. Please refer to the individual registration page or written communication for specific cancellation policy details.

If no specific cancellation policy is stated for an event, meeting, or conference, the program cancellation table in Section 4 applies.

No refunds or credits are issued for no-shows, late arrivals, early departures, weather concerns, personal scheduling conflicts, or failure to attend an Offering that proceeds as scheduled.

---

## 8. Association Cancellations and Changes

The Association may cancel, postpone, modify, relocate, reschedule, or change the format of an Offering due to attendance, speaker availability, venue issues, weather, technology issues, public health concerns, or other circumstances.

If the Association cancels an Offering after payment has been completed, the Association may, at its discretion, issue a refund of the paid fee, provide an account credit, or offer a transfer to another eligible Offering.

The Association is not responsible for travel, lodging, parking, meals, lost time, or other incidental expenses.

---

## 9. Transfers

Transfers are subject to availability, eligibility requirements, membership status, price differences, program prerequisites, and Association approval.

If a transfer is approved, the Association may require payment of any price difference, processing fee, or administrative fee before completing the transfer.

---

## 10. Payment Processing Fees

Third-party payment processors may charge nonrefundable processing or transaction fees. If a refund is issued, the Association may exclude processing fees, transaction fees, wire fees, and other third-party fees from the refunded amount unless required by law or expressly stated otherwise.

---

## 11. Removals for Conduct Violations

Participants removed from an Offering due to violations of the Code of Conduct, Terms of Participation, applicable policies, or applicable laws are not eligible for refunds, credits, or transfers.

---

## 12. Chargebacks and Payment Disputes

Initiating a chargeback or payment dispute without first contacting the Association may result in suspension or removal from current or future Association Activities.

Chargebacks inconsistent with this policy may be treated as violations of the Terms of Participation, and the Association reserves the right to contest such disputes.

---

## 13. Policy Changes

The version of this Refund and Cancellation Policy in effect at the time of purchase or registration applies to that Offering unless the Offering states otherwise.

The Association may update this policy from time to time.
