# Membership

Membership

* Individual membership fees are nonrefundable after purchase.

Individual Membership

**Non-Transferrable.** Membership in Carolina Compensation and Rewards Association (CCRA)  is managed on an individual basis and cannot be transferred to another person. Use of your membership by anyone other than you is a violation of the CCRA membership policy.

**Auto-Renewal.** If you have elected to auto-renew your membership, your membership will automatically renew on the membership renewal date for the same period as your original membership period purchase. The membership renewal fee will automatically be charged to your credit card on file on your membership renewal date.

**Cancellation.** If cancelled, CCRA membership is nonrefundable. For memberships with auto-renewal, you will need to cancel before your membership renewal date to avoid payment of the membership renewal fee.

**Duration.** Membership is valid for 12 months from the last day of the month in which payment was received. For example, if payment is received on 11/1/2022, that membership will expire on 11/30/2023.

**Continuation.** Because we value your engagement, your WorldatWork membership will follow you even if you leave the organization that paid for your membership\! To ensure that you retain access to your membership account, we encourage you to update your email preferences, including your log-in user name. Please contact our Customer Experience Team if you experience any issues with updating your records.

**Discounts.** CCRA offers discounts to qualified individuals, such as WorldatWork members that list CCRA as their local affiliate chapter, and undergraduate students.  For more information and to see if you qualify, contact our Director of Membership at **Email**.
